Contact

Say hi.

GSS is made in the open: questions, bugs, ideas and scenes all have a place, on X or on GitHub. There is no sales team and no form to fill in.

Questions and scenes

Ask a question, or show a scene you made with GSS, on X: @GSS_lang. A link to the playground with your scene in it is the quickest way to show it.

Bugs and ideas

Report a bug or suggest a feature in the issues on GitHub. For a bug, the smallest scene that shows it, the browser and whether it runs on WebGL2 or WebGPU help the most.

Contributing

Read the contributing guide, then open a pull request. Everyone whose work is in GSS is thanked on the home page.

The person behind it

GSS is made by Lucas Bonomi, on GitHub as @LukyVj. What the site collects when you visit it is on the privacy page.
