Privacy

What this site keeps.

No account, no cookies, no advertising. The site counts its visits without knowing who you are, and a few pages call other services, listed here.

Visit counts

Every page loads Umami, which counts the pages seen, where the visit came from, the browser, the country and the size of the screen. It sets no cookie and keeps no profile of a visitor. It also counts a few actions: sharing a scene from the playground, opening an example, trying an example of the docs. A blocker of trackers stops it, and the site works the same.

Search in the docs

The search of the docs is Algolia DocSearch: what you type is sent to Algolia to find the pages. Your recent searches stay in your browser.

What stays in your browser

The site remembers a few choices in the localStorage of your browser, never sent anywhere: the width of the panels of the playground and of the search, the resolution of the scenes, whether the profiler is open, the view of the showcase. Clearing the data of the site in your browser forgets them.

Other services

  • The site is hosted by Vercel, whose servers log each request, like any web server.
  • The avatars of the contributors, on the home page, come from GitHub.
  • The films of the brand page come from media.gss-lang.dev, on Cloudflare, and load only when you play one.
