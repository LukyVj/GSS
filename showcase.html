Showcase

What you can make with GSS.

Real uses, for designers, creative coders and developers. Every scene on this page is live: drag it, hover it, open it in the playground. And every one is embedded with <gss-scene>, the same tag you can put on your own site.

01For designers

Your logo, your icons, your tokens: the things you already draw in 2D, in 3D, written like a stylesheet.

02For creative coders

Rules instead of loops, and a readable way into the shader: every scene becomes GLSL you can read, and export.

03For developers

A 3D scene is a stylesheet: small enough to ship, simple enough for an LLM to write.

04On your site

Three ways to put a scene on a page, from a tag you paste to a build step that keeps the compiler out of the page.

noteGSS has no transparency yet: give the scene the background of your page. A browser keeps about 16 WebGL contexts; a scene only starts when it comes into view, and sleeps when it leaves it.

05Inspiration

Scenes to start from. Each one opens in the playground, where you can change it and share it with a link.
